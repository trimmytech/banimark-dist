# Banimark

**A self-hosted AI support desk for your website or app.** It runs on your own
server, uses your own AI key, and answers your customers from your own data.
Conversations and customer details never leave your infrastructure.

Banimark handles the routine questions on its own, looks things up in your records
when a customer asks, and hands the tricky ones to your team through a live inbox.
It works around the clock, in the languages your customers use.

## What you get

- **Answers from your own data.** You decide, in plain language, what it may look
  up. It can never see more than you allow, and every customer only ever sees
  their own information.
- **A real handover to people.** When a conversation needs a human, your team
  takes over in a live inbox, with email alerts so nothing is missed.
- **Your customers' privacy kept.** Nothing is sent to us. You bring your own AI
  provider and key, and the data stays on your server.
- **One chat, everywhere.** A website widget, a shareable chat link, and a native
  mobile app — all from the same settings.
- **A free trial to start.** Try it, then a licence keeps your updates and support
  coming. If a licence ever lapses, your chat still keeps serving visitors.

## Once it's running

Everything is set up from the admin panel — no coding:

- **Teach it your lookups** — "find this customer's orders", "check a ticket" — in
  a few clicks.
- **Shape how it talks** with plain-sentence rules, such as "never promise a
  refund date".
- **Add your team**, each with their own login and optional two-factor security.
- **Make it yours** — colour, logo, greeting, and where it appears — with a live
  preview and a test page.
- **See how you're doing** — a dashboard of conversations, response times, and
  AI-written insights into what your customers are asking for.
